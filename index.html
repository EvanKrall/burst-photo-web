          This is a port of Burst Photo that runs in your browser, using WebGPU for acceleration. See https://burst.photo/tech/ for a nice explanation of how the algorithms work.
          For processing of camera raw files, this uses FlashRaw.
        

        

          This port is vibecoded. If you prefer human-written code, I recommend the original Burst Photo, but unfortunately it's Mac-only.
        

        

          Your photos are processed entirely within your browser, rather than being uploaded to the cloud.
